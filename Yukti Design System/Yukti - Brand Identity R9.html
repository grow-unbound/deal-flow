The Voussoir.
Refined, not reinvented.

The eight-round exploration is closed. R6 · 07 — The Voussoir earned the strongest meaning-to-mark fit: three cut stones forming a flat arch, the keystone seating the structure that lets the rest hold. This book optimizes that exact mark and builds the system around it — geometry, wordmark, spacing, color, and the first artifacts.

Mark · R6-07 Voussoir Wordmark · Mukta / Baloo 2 Primary · Charcoal + Copper Secondary · Deep Navy + Signal Orange
01

The mark

A flat arch of three voussoirs. The projecting keystone is the piece Yukti supplies — the wedge that lets the two haunches bear and hold. Read it as masonry: cut, seated, structural.

Primary mark · charcoal field · copper keystone
Meaning

The keystone holds

The one wedge cut to receive load and pass it on. Yukti is the part that makes the structure stand — not the ornament on top of it.

Register

Structural, foundational

Architectural, not heraldic. It is built from mass and seam, engineered to survive at favicon size and hold in a single color.

Accent law

Copper marks the load

Colour is structural only: the accent isolates the load-bearing keystone. It never decorates and never crowns.

02

Construction & anatomy

Three trapezoidal blocks radiating from a centre of curvature below the mark. Flat crown, radial seams, splayed haunches — the cues that keep it reading as an arch and never as a crown.

01

Keystone (projecting)

The central wedge sits proud of the haunches with a flat, wide top — a cut stone, never a point or finial.

02

Haunches (springers)

Two blocks splay down and outward, lower than the keystone, leaning on it like the springing stones of a flat arch.

03

Radial seams

The mortar joints align to one centre below the mark, so the three blocks read as a single jointed structure.

04

Square aperture

The mark occupies a 32-unit square, vertically centred. Bounds: keystone top at the crown, haunch base on the springing line.

03

Clear space & minimum size

Protect the mark with breathing room equal to the keystone’s crown width (X). Never reproduce it below its floor sizes.

Minimum size

Clear space = X, the width of the keystone crown, held on all four sides.
Digital floor · 16 px for the mark, 20 px cap-height for the horizontal lockup.
Print floor · 8 mm mark height; keep seams open at all sizes.

04

Wordmark & lockups

Mukta is the primary wordmark — institutional, even, low-contrast, credible from body copy to signage. Baloo 2 is the secondary cut, a rounder register for app-forward and friendlier surfaces. The mark sits square to the cap height; the wordmark is always lowercase.

05

Colour system

Two systems. Charcoal + Copper is the primary identity — grounded, material, structural. Deep Navy + Signal Orange is the secondary, for product surfaces and high-signal moments.

06

Monochrome & reversal

The mark holds in one colour. Positive on light, reversed on dark, and a single-weight knockout for stamping, etching, and fax-grade reproduction.

07

Light & dark lockups

The full lockup in both modes, each palette. On dark, copper lifts to #D9894C and orange to #FF7A33 to hold contrast.

08

Favicon & app icon

At icon scale the three blocks nearly touch and read as one seated arch. The app icon seats the mark in a charcoal rounded square; the favicon is the bare mark.

Misuse — hold the line

The single biggest risk is drift into heraldry. The Voussoir is structure, not status. Never let it become any of these.
