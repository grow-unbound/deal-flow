03

Construction & anatomy

The frozen geometry. Three trapezoidal blocks radiating from a centre of curvature below the mark — flat crown, radial seams, splayed haunches. These proportions do not change.

01

Keystone (projecting)

The central wedge sits proud of the haunches with a flat, wide top — a cut stone, never a point or finial.

02

Haunches (springers)

Two blocks splay down and outward, lower than the keystone, leaning on it like the springing stones of a flat arch.

03

Radial seams

The mortar joints align to one centre below the mark, so the three blocks read as a single jointed structure.

04

Square aperture

The mark occupies a 32-unit square, vertically centred. Bounds: keystone top at the crown, haunch base on the springing line.

04

06

Colour system

One palette: Charcoal + Copper. Charcoal does the work — a near-black ink for text and a lighter surface for large dark areas. Copper is reserved for the mark and accents, never body text.

07

Monochrome & reversal

Because the default mark is already one colour, reproduction is simple. Copper positive, copper reversed, and a single-weight charcoal knockout for stamping, etching and fax-grade output.

08

Light & dark

Light is canonical and shown first; dark is a supported variant. The full lockup in both modes — on dark, copper lifts to #D9894C to hold contrast and the ground is charcoal surface, not pure black.

10

Accessibility — built in

WCAG AA throughout. Copper is never used as text. And no status or signal is ever carried by colour alone — it is always paired with a shape and a label, so it reads for colourblind users (≈8%) and everyone else.

Text contrast

Charcoal ink on paper clears AA and AAA. Copper as text fails AA — which is exactly why it is reserved for the mark and accents.

Never colour alone

Status is encoded by shape + label first, colour second. Remove the colour and every state still reads.

Each state carries a distinct glyph — check, triangle, square, ring — so it survives greyscale, low vision and every form of colour-blindness.

11

Misuse — hold the line

The biggest risks: drift into heraldry, and breaking the single-colour rule. The Voussoir is structure, not status. Never let it become any of these.

12

The system in use, led in light mode — enough to brief production. Single-colour copper carries the identity; the wordmark is Baloo 2; the mark seats tight to the word.
