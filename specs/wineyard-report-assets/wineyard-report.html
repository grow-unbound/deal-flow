Go-live briefing · 90-day window ending 2026-07-24 · Source: DealFlow production metrics

WineYard did ₹9.42 Cr of invoiced business in 90 days across 2,811 active buyers and 8 locations — but the buyer app is only touching ~2.8% of that revenue today. Not a catalog problem: buyers with access can already browse and order the full 340-product active catalog. It's an access problem — only 171 of 10,909 buyers have the app enabled. Same business, more of it moving through a channel you control instead of paper and phone calls, once rollout catches up.

Everything above came from the same tables the dashboard already reads. The split below is what's a display task vs. what's a genuine roadmap item.

Available today no new build

  • Revenue / estimate / order trends, tenant-wide and per-location
