## Design direction (from Yukti_DesignSystem_R12 + Brand Brief — binding)

- **Palette:** Charcoal `#221E1A` + Copper `#B5642F` only. Paper ground `#F8F6F2`, canvas `#FCFBF8`, hairlines `#EAE3D9`. Light mode. Copper = accents; **≤1 copper CTA per screen** (the demo CTA). All other buttons charcoal. Copper never as body text.
- **Type:** Inter (400–800) for everything; page hero ~Inter 800, -0.02em. Baloo 2 **only** in the wordmark. JetBrains Mono only for numbers meant to be scanned (stats, ₹ figures). Tabular numerals for all money.
- **Mark:** Voussoir keystone, single-colour copper. Two-tone allowed in the hero only. Tagline lockup: "The keystone of your business" — always paired with the plain descriptor.
- **Imagery:** real operators and real product UI. Masonry/structural motifs welcome. **Never:** ledgers, calculators, coins, stamps, paperwork, glowing-AI gimmicks, enterprise-blue gloss.
- **Voice:** short sentences, active, concrete. Bold about growth; precise about money. No "AI," "smart," "automate," "leverage," "edge" as lead words. No exclamation marks.
- **Status/step graphics:** shape + label, never colour alone.
- **Numbers style:** lakh/crore formatting, ₹ proportional and hugging.

### 2. The problem (empathy section)

**H2:** You built a real business. Your tools didn't keep up.

Three cards (shape-glyph icon + short copy, not colour-coded):

1. **Fifteen portals, zero picture.** Every brand gives you its own portal, catalog, and price file. Nobody gives you one view of *your* business.
2. **Orders die in WhatsApp.** Enquiries buried in group chats and voice notes. Two to four out of every ten never become orders.
3. **Prices live in your head.** Special rates per retailer, in spreadsheets and memory. Every manual quote risks margin.

**Closer line:** Yukti was built from inside this problem — for the distributor, not for the brands.

### 3. How it works (the golden path — 3 steps)

**H2:** From price decision to placed order, in minutes.

Numbered steps (large numerals, product screenshots per step):

1. **Build a campaign.** Pick products, set the campaign price, choose the retailer group, set validity. One screen. See the average discount before you publish.
2. **Send it to WhatsApp.** Every targeted retailer gets the campaign on their own WhatsApp — individually, tracked, opt-out respected. Not lost in group noise.
3. **Orders come to you.** Retailers tap the link, verify with an OTP, and order from a clean price list made for them. No app install, no password. Every order lands in your dashboard.

**Proof line under steps:** Then watch the funnel: Sent → Delivered → Opened → Ordered. You'll know exactly who to call.

### 4. Feature grid (six tiles)

**H2:** One place to run the whole operation.

1. **Campaigns** — Special pricing, for select products, for a chosen group of retailers, for a limited time. Built in one flow, previewed exactly as buyers see it.
2. **WhatsApp broadcast** — Message everyone in a city, everyone who hasn't ordered in 30 days, or everyone with dues — computed from your own data, sent one-to-one, fully tracked.
3. **Buyer app your retailers will actually use** — A link, an OTP, a personal price list. Their prices, your brands, no install.
4. **Customer groups & pricing** — A-class, B-class, city-wise, brand-wise. Set the price once per group; every quote and order follows it. No leakage.
5. **Orders in one flow** — Enquiry → order → confirmed → dispatched → delivered. Status at a glance, invoice PDF in one click.
6. **Your books stay clean** — Orders and invoices flow to Tally and Zoho. Your accountant gets structured data, not re-typing.

### 9. FAQ (accordion)

1. **Do my retailers need to install an app?** No. They open a WhatsApp link, verify with an OTP, and order from the browser. It works on any phone.
2. **Does it work with Tally?** Yes — CSV exports for items, sales vouchers, and ledgers that import cleanly. Zoho Books and Zoho Inventory sync directly.
3. **Can I message my existing WhatsApp groups?** No — and that's deliberate. Yukti messages each retailer individually so you can see delivery, opens, and orders per person. Groups can't do that. Retailers can opt out anytime.
4. **What does it cost?** Three plans — Starter, Growth, Scale — sized to your team and brand count. Talk to us; most distributors compare it to a fraction of one salesperson's salary. WhatsApp messages run on prepaid credits, so you pay for what you send.
5. **How long does setup take?** Most distributors publish their first campaign within two weeks, with our team migrating your products and customers from Tally, Zoho, or Excel.
6. **Is my data safe? Can other distributors see it?** Your data is yours alone. Every account is fully isolated, and your retailers see only what you publish to them.

## Copy rules for the designer/builder (enforce)

1. ≤1 copper CTA per viewport; all secondary buttons charcoal.
2. Never over-promise on money/accuracy: allowed ceiling is "your books stay clean," "structured data," "deterministic pricing." Banned: "error-free," "books keep themselves," "replaces Tally/ERP," "automates your accounting."
3. No "AI" anywhere on this site.
4. Retailer-facing claims always include the no-install, no-password, opt-out-anytime trio — it's both the honest pitch and the compliance posture.
5. All stats in JetBrains Mono with tabular numerals; everything else Inter.
6. Every section ends within one scroll of a demo CTA.
